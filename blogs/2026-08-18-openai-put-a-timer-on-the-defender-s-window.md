Yesterday, OpenAI published "The Defender's Window," a post by Greg Brockman. His argument: AI models can now automate parts of real-world cyberattacks, open weight models with similar capabilities are only months behind the frontier, and defenders have a limited window to use these same capabilities to get ahead.
